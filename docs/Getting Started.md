# Stirling PDF documentation

<p class="lead">Stirling is a private, open-source PDF editor and processor for you and your agents. Read, edit and automate PDFs in your browser, on your own computer or on your own servers.</p>

<h2 class="section-label">Pick your setup</h2>

<Cards cols="3">
<Card title="Cloud" icon="cloud" accent="cloud" href="./Cloud/Cloud.md">
Stirling Cloud, in your browser with nothing to install. The quickest way to start.
</Card>
<Card title="Desktop" icon="desktop" accent="desktop" href="./Desktop/Desktop.md">
Install the editor on your own computer, on Windows, Mac and Linux. Works offline.
</Card>
<Card title="Server" icon="server" accent="server" href="./Server/Server.md">
Run Stirling on your own infrastructure, from a container to bare metal. Best for production and growing organisations.
</Card>
</Cards>
