# Stirling Processor

Stirling Processor runs PDF workflows for you, in bulk or as new files arrive: **source → steps → destination**.

![Processor home: documents from five sources flowing through five policies, with 8,788 delivered and 272 failed in 24 hours](/img/processor/processor-home.gif)

Not in the desktop app; see [Setup and access](./Setup-and-Access.md).

## Start here

Follow [Create your first pipeline](./Getting-Started.md) to build, test and switch on a compression pipeline.
