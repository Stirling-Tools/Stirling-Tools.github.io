# Account and sign-in

## Sign in

Go to [stirling.com/app](https://stirling.com/app) and enter your email and password, or choose **Sign in with Google**, **Sign in with GitHub** or **Use magic link instead** (Stirling emails you a sign-in link).

To use the same account in the desktop app, see [Connection modes](../Desktop/Connection-Modes.md).

## Create an account

Select **Create an account** on the sign-in page. Sign up with Google or GitHub, or with your email and a password of at least 6 characters, then open the confirmation link if one is emailed to you.

## Reset your password

Select **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page. Enter your email and select **Send reset link**, then follow the link in the email.

## Use Stirling as a guest

Select **Skip →** on the sign-in page. Guests can use every manual PDF tool. AI tools, automation, the Processor, API keys, MCP and saving files to Stirling Cloud need an account.

To keep a guest account, open **Settings → Account Settings → Overview**, fill in **Upgrade Guest Account** and select **Upgrade Account**.

## Change your password

Open **Settings → Account Settings → Passwords & Security** and select **Change password**.

## Sign out

Open **Settings → Account Settings → Overview** and select **Log out**.

## Delete your account

Open **Settings → Account Settings → Overview**, select **Delete Account**, type your email address and select **Delete My Account**. Deletion is permanent. Guest accounts do not have this option.
