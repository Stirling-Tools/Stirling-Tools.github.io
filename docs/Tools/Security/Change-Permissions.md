# Change Permissions

**Change Permissions** restricts what people can do with a PDF, like printing, copying or editing.

1. Open **Change Permissions** and add your PDF.
2. Tick each action to block:
   - **Prevent printing**, or **Prevent printing different formats** to allow only low-quality printing.
   - **Prevent content extraction** stops copying text and images. **Prevent extraction for accessibility** also blocks screen readers.
   - **Prevent modification** and **Prevent annotation modification** block editing the content and the comments.
   - **Prevent filling in form** locks form fields, and **Prevent assembly of document** blocks adding, removing or rotating pages.
3. Click **Change Permissions**.
