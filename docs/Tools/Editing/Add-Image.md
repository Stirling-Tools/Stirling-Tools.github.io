# Add image

**Add image** places a logo, stamp or photo anywhere on a PDF.

<Video id="_bUEZPrUjoM" title="Add an image to a PDF" />

1. Open **Add image** and add your PDF. It opens in the viewer.
2. Click **Select image file** and choose a PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, TIFF or SVG file.
3. Click the page to place it. Each click adds another copy, until you click **Pause placement**.
4. Click an image to select it. Drag a corner to resize it, or drag the middle to move it.
5. **Remove white background** makes white areas see-through, which suits stamps and scanned signatures.
6. Click **Apply Images** to save them into the PDF.

To move or replace images already in the PDF, use [PDF Text Editor](./PDF-Text-Editor.md).
