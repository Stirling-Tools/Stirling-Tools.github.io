# Extract Images

**Extract Images** saves every picture in a PDF as its own image file.

1. Open **Extract Images** and add your PDF.
2. Choose the format: **PNG** keeps full quality and transparency, **JPG** gives smaller files that suit photos, or **GIF**.
3. An image used on several pages, like a logo, is saved once. Tick **Save duplicate images** to keep the repeats.
4. Click **Extract**. Each image is named after the page it came from.

A few images open as separate files and more arrive as one ZIP. Change the limit in **Settings**, under **Downloads**.
