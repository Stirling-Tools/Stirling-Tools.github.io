# Add Stamp to PDF

**Add Stamp to PDF** places text, an image or a QR code at one spot on the pages you choose.

1. Open **Add Stamp to PDF** and add your PDF.
2. Under **Custom Page Selection**, choose the pages, for example `1,3`, `2-5` or `all`.
3. Choose a **Stamp Type**:
   - **Text** - type the stamp, or use **Use Template** for a common one such as page numbers. Stamp text can include dynamic variables such as the page number, the date or the file name.
   - **Image** - click **Choose File** and pick a logo or signature.
4. Open **Position & Formatting** and click a spot on the preview, or drag the stamp to a custom position. Set its rotation, opacity, size, colour and margin.
5. Click **Submit**.
