# Compare

Compare shows the wording changes between two versions of a PDF. Removed words are highlighted in red and added words in green. The comparison runs on your device, so the files are not uploaded.

## Compare two PDFs

1. Open **Compare**.
2. Under **Original PDF**, pick the older version.
3. Under **Edited PDF**, pick the newer version. Use **Swap** if you picked them the wrong way round.
4. Click **Compare**.

## Review the result

- **Place side by side** / **Stack vertically** switch the layout of the two panes.
- **Link scroll** keeps both panes in step. **Unlink scroll** lets you scroll and pan each pane on its own.
- The **Additions** and **Deletions** dropdowns list every change. Pick one to jump to it, or search the list.
- Zoom with the scroll wheel or a pinch, and drag to pan.

## Limitations

- Compare checks extracted text only. Layout, formatting and image changes are not detected.
- A PDF with no text layer can't be compared. For scanned documents, run [OCR](./OCR.md) on both files first.
