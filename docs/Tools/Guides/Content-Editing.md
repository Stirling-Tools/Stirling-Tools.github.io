# Content and editing

## PDF Text Editor

**PDF Text Editor** lets you edit the existing text and images of a PDF. Open it from the tool picker, or click **Edit Text** in the viewer's top bar. Click **Save PDF** to apply your changes to the file in your workspace, or **Download** to save a copy.

- **Font family** lists the matched font, included fonts, **Server fonts** your admin added, and fonts on your device.
- On scanned pages, typed text uses the closest matching font. To pick another, click **Font not looking right?**
- **Ctrl + F** opens find and replace.

<Video id="T9tGn7lh7p4" title="Edit text in a PDF" />

### Scanned pages

A scanned page has no text to edit until it's been through OCR. Click **Run OCR** in the panel, then click into any line to correct it. Only the words you change are redrawn.

<Video id="Cf2GfQDfq7Q" title="Edit text in a scanned PDF" />

### Tables

The editor outlines text that lines up in rows and columns. Click **Edit** on a table to change cells, add or delete rows and columns, and resize or move it. **Add table** starts a new one.

<Video id="VanKkhhhFJI" title="Edit tables in a PDF" />

### Images and objects

Click an image to select it, or click **Image** in the top bar to add one. Drag to move and resize it, and use the **Selected** panel to rotate, flip, replace or arrange it.

<Video id="fK1CB_82NpY" title="Add, move and resize images in a PDF" />

## Images

- **Add image** - upload a PNG, JPEG, GIF, BMP, TIFF or SVG file, then click anywhere on the page to place it. Click **Apply Images** when you're done.
- **Extract Images** - saves every image in the PDF to a ZIP file. Choose the image format to convert them to, and tick **Save duplicate images** to keep repeats.

To split a scan of several photos into separate images, use [Detect & Split Scanned Photos](./Advanced-Tools.md#detect-split-scanned-photos).

<Video id="_bUEZPrUjoM" title="Add an image to a PDF" />

## Stamps & annotations

- **Add Text** - type your text, click on the page to place it, then click **Apply Text**.
- **Add Stamp to PDF** - add a text, image or QR code stamp. Set its position (or drag it in the preview), size, rotation, opacity and margin. Stamp text can include dynamic variables such as the current date, the file name or the page number.
- **Annotate** - highlight, draw, and add notes and shapes in the viewer. See [Read and annotate](../Workspace/Read-and-Annotate.md).

<Video id="fMZ6kDW_dZI" title="Add text to a PDF" />

## Remove content

- **Remove image** - strips every image from the PDF.
- **Remove Annotations** - deletes all comments, highlights and markup.

<Video id="duLM1nayu4E" title="Remove images" />

## Document details

- **Change Metadata** - edit the standard fields (author, title, subject, keywords, creator, producer, creation and modification dates), add your own fields under **Custom Metadata**, or tick **Delete all metadata** to strip them.
- **Edit Table of Contents** - add, nest and reorder bookmarks in the **Bookmark editor**. Click **Load from selected PDF** to start from the existing bookmarks, and use **Import JSON** or **Download JSON** to reuse an outline. Click **Apply table of contents** to save.
