# Mobile scanner

## Scan from your phone

1. On your computer, click the QR code button next to **Add Files** (**Open Files** in the desktop app). Its tooltip is **Upload from Mobile**. In the file picker, the same option is called **From your phone**.
2. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera and open the link.
3. Choose an upload method:
   - **Camera** - photograph each page. Edge detection finds the page and crops it for you. Use **Retake** or **Add More** as needed.
   - **File Upload** - pick existing photos on the phone.
4. Tap **Upload**. The files appear on your computer, and you can close the tab on your phone.

- **Camera** needs a secure (HTTPS) connection. If the phone says camera access requires HTTPS, use **File Upload** instead, or ask your admin to serve Stirling PDF over HTTPS.
- The QR code button is hidden when you open Stirling PDF on a phone. If you don't see it on a computer, the mobile scanner is turned off. On a self-hosted server an admin can turn it on ([Mobile scanner settings](../../Server/Customisation/Mobile-Scanner.md)).

## In the desktop app

- Your phone and computer must be on the same network (Wi-Fi).
- The phone page has no edge detection or auto-crop, so crop your photos before uploading.
- The phone page also accepts PDFs.
