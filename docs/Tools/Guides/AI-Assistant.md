# AI assistant

The AI assistant answers questions about the files in your workspace and runs tools for you when you describe what you want in plain words.

## Where it's available

- **Stirling Cloud:** included for signed-in accounts. AI use draws on your team's credits ([Plans, credits and billing](../../Cloud/Plans-and-Billing.md)). Guests are asked to create an account.
- **Desktop app:** sign in to Stirling Cloud, or connect to a self-hosted server that has AI turned on ([Connection modes](../../Desktop/Connection-Modes.md)). It isn't available in Local Only mode.
- **Self-hosted server:** AI is off until an admin sets it up ([AI on your server](../../Server/AI/AI.md)). The admin can also turn off individual capabilities, so you may not see all of them.

## Open the assistant

1. Add your files to the workspace.
2. Click **Open Stirling AI assistant** at the bottom right. The **AI Assistant** panel opens.
3. Type in **What do you want to do?** and click **Send message**, or pick a suggestion under **Get started**, such as **Compress this document**.

The assistant works on the files in your workspace. When it changes a file, the result is saved as a new version of that file. Merged, split or new files are added to the workspace as new files.

## What it can do

| Capability | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Chat assistant** | Runs tools when you ask, for example "merge these into one PDF" or "convert this to PDF". |
| **Document questions** | Answers questions about the content of a PDF, for example "what's the payment deadline?" |
| **Math auditor** | Checks a document for calculation errors, such as table totals that don't add up. |
| **PDF comment agent** | Adds review comments to a PDF based on your instructions. |
| **Create PDF from prompt** | Creates a new PDF from a description. |

For what AI sends on Stirling Cloud, see [Files and privacy](../../Cloud/Files-and-Privacy.md#what-ai-tools-send).
