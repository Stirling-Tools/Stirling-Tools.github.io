# Crop PDF

**Crop PDF** trims pages down to just the area you want to keep.

<Video id="uG6FAbmHVIw" title="Crop pages" />

## Crop by hand

1. Open **Crop PDF** and add your file. The preview shows the first page with a crop box around it.
2. Drag the corner handles to frame the area to keep. Drag inside the box to move it.
3. For an exact size, type values under **Position** and **Size**, in points measured from the bottom left.
4. Under **Pages to crop**, enter page numbers such as `1,3,5-8`, or `all`.
5. Click **Apply Crop**.

## Crop automatically

Click **Auto-crop whitespace** to find the content on each page and trim the empty margins.
