# Multi-Page Layout

**Multi-Page Layout** fits several pages onto each sheet, for handouts or to save paper.

1. Open **Multi-Page Layout** and add your file.
2. Under **Pages per sheet**, choose 2, 4, 9 or 16, or choose **Custom** and set **Rows** and **Columns**. The preview shows where each page lands.
3. Under **Advanced settings**, set:
   - **Orientation** - landscape suits wide pages, like slides.
   - **Page arrangement** - fill the grid by columns or by rows. **Reading Direction** can run right to left.
   - **Margins and borders** - space around the sheet and between pages, in points. **Add Borders** draws a line around each page, with **Border Thickness** for its weight.
4. Click **Submit**.

To print pages as a folded booklet, use [Booklet Imposition](./Booklet.md).
