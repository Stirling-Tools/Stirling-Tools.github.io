# Reorganize Pages

**Reorganize Pages** puts the pages of a PDF in a new order.

<Video id="v05M7TE0YIg" title="Reorder PDF pages" />

## Set your own order

1. Open **Reorganize Pages** and add your file.
2. Under **Custom Page Order**, list the pages in their new order, separated by commas. Ranges such as `5-8` work too, for example `1,5-8,2-4`. Pages you leave out are dropped, so list every page you want to keep.
3. Click **Reorganize Pages**.

## Ready-made orders

Choose an **Organization mode** instead of a custom order:

- **Reverse Order** - the last page comes first.
- **Duplex Sort** - fixes double-sided pages scanned one side at a time: all the fronts, then the backs in reverse.
- **Odd-Even Split** - all the odd pages first, then all the even ones.
- **Odd-Even Merge** and **Duplicate all pages** are also available.

To move pages by dragging thumbnails, use the [Page Editor](../Workspace/Page-Editor.md).
