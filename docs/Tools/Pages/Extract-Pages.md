# Extract Pages

**Extract Pages** keeps only the pages you choose, in their original order, for example to send part of a long document.

1. Open **Extract Pages** and add your PDF.
2. Under **Pages to Extract**, enter the pages:
   - single pages separated by commas, and ranges such as `1,3,5-8`
   - formulas such as `2n-1` for odd pages or `3n` for every third page
   - combine rules with `&`, and exclude pages with `!`, for example even pages except 6
3. Click **Extract Pages**.

The result replaces the file in your workspace as a new version.
