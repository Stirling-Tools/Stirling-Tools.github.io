# Booklet Imposition

**Booklet Imposition** arranges your pages so a printed stack folds into a booklet that reads in order. It puts two pages side by side on each sheet.

<Video id="2lLef7sfHRQ" title="Print a folded booklet" />

1. Open **Booklet Imposition** and add your file.
2. **Double-sided printing** is on by default, for printers that print both sides. Without one, untick it for **Manual Duplex Mode**: print the **1st Pass**, put the stack back in face down, then print the **2nd Pass** on the backs.
3. Under **Advanced Options**, set any of:
   - **Right-to-left binding** - puts the spine on the right, for languages like Arabic or Hebrew.
   - **Add borders around pages** - draws lines to help with cutting and alignment.
   - **Flip on short edge** - matches duplex printers that flip that way.
4. Click **Create Booklet**. The paper size comes from your first page.

Print the result double-sided, fold the stack in half and staple along the fold.
