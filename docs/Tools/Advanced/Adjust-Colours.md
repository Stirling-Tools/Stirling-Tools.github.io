# Adjust Colors/Contrast

**Adjust Colors/Contrast** brings faded scans and dull pages back to life.

<Video id="n_el2UTahSQ" title="Adjust colours and contrast" />

1. Open **Adjust Colors/Contrast** and add your file. The preview shows the first page and updates as you change each setting.
2. Adjust:
   - **Contrast** - makes darks darker and lights lighter. Raise it to make faded text stand out.
   - **Brightness** - lightens or darkens the whole page.
   - **Saturation** - makes colours stronger or greyer.
   - **Red**, **green** and **blue** levels - remove a colour cast, like yellowed paper.
3. Click **Confirm**.

Each page is rebuilt as an image, so its text can no longer be selected.
