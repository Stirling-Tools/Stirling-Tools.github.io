# Replace & Invert Color

**Replace & Invert Color** recolours a PDF's text and background, for easier reading or for print.

1. Open **Replace & Invert Color** and add your file.
2. Under **Color operation**, choose:
   - **High contrast** - a ready-made pair, such as white or yellow text on black.
   - **Custom** - pick your own text and background colours.
   - **Invert all colors** - a negative of each page, for example for a dark-mode copy. The text can no longer be selected.
   - **Convert to CMYK** - switches the colours to the ink model used for professional printing.
3. Click **Replace**.
