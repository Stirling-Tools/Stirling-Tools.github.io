# Detect & Split Scanned Photos

**Detect & Split Scanned Photos** finds each photo on a scanned page and saves it on its own.

<Video id="NyJS90M1080" title="Split scanned photos into separate images" />

1. Scan your photos on a plain, light background, with a small gap between them.
2. Open **Detect & Split Scanned Photos** and add the scan.
3. Adjust the settings if needed:
   - **Tolerance** - how far a colour can differ from the background and still count as background. Raise it for uneven backgrounds.
   - **Angle Threshold** - straightens photos tilted by at least that many degrees.
   - **Border Size** - trims that many pixels from the edge of each photo.
4. Click **Extract Image Scans**. Each photo is cropped out and saved as its own PNG image.
